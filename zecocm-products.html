TRACE — what the AI layer does

Cited or refused. Nothing in between.

TRACE only reads what the requesting user is already authorized to read, and it only speaks when the record supports it.

Evidence-grounded answers
Every substantive answer is derived from project records the requesting user is authorized to see — never from outside knowledge.
Citation required
An answer is not presented as supported unless it names the evidence behind it. Enforced in the system, not left to a reviewer to catch.
Refusal state
When the record is insufficient, contradictory, or outside TRACE's authority to speak on, it says so. "No match found" is never presented as "no requirement exists."
Authorization-scoped retrieval
TRACE retrieves only what the asking user already has access to — nothing is inferred from a broader grant elsewhere in the project.
Historical context
A conclusion is tied to the evidence and authority state that existed at the time in question, not just the record as it looks today.
Self-observing, not self-modifying
Escalations and refusals are surfaced for human product review. TRACE cannot grant itself new authority or change its own governing rules.

What TRACE does not do

  • Does not replace professional, contractual, or legal judgment — material decisions still need confirmation against the cited source.
  • Does not read past what the requesting user is authorized to see.
  • Does not treat an empty search result as proof that no requirement exists.
  • Does not silently alter the governed project record — corrections supersede, they don't overwrite.
  • Does not change its own protected behavior, with or without being asked.

Evaluating ZecoCM for public procurement

What a reviewer usually asks for first.

  • Security posture: tenant isolation, encryption in transit, single-region infrastructure — documented, including what's not yet certified.
  • AI governance: how TRACE is scoped, what it refuses, and how escalations reach a human reviewer.
  • Data ownership: you can export the record, provenance, hashes, and verification package without ZecoCM's involvement.
  • Retention: governed history — corrections supersede, nothing is silently rewritten.
  • Terms & procurement contact: for RFP language, insurance, and vendor documentation.
  • Who you're actually dealing with: a real registered office and a direct line — not a form that disappears into a queue.

Security & trust

Built this way, not certified this way — yet.

The record is honest about where ZecoCM stands today: no SOC 2, FedRAMP, or independent penetration test has been completed. Real architectural controls exist now; the compliance program is still being built. A reviewer should hear that from us before they find it themselves.
